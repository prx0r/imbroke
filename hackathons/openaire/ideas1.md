## Why GraphGit isn't built yet

It partially is. Patala has append-only events, Merkle checkpoints, `/v1/changes`. But the "git for knowledge graphs" framing doesn't exist in code. It's a naming gap.

NOT crypto people. NOT token economies.

Scholar bounty model is real architecture but:
1. Future concept, not hackathon demo
2. Economically unclear (who pays?)
3. Alienating to academics
